New to entropy?
  • Every coin owns an entropy: the average surprise one flip delivers, in bits.
  • A fair coin's entropy is exactly 1 bit; a two-headed coin's is 0.
  • A coin's entropy is also the limit of compressing its flips — the reason ZIP files work.
  • Start at the source: Shannon's A Mathematical Theory of Communication (1948).

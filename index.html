In one 1948 paper, Claude Shannon (1916–2001) worked out how to measure information itself. A Mathematical Theory of Communication gave us the bit, entropy, and channel capacity — the mathematical bedrock under every file ever compressed and every message ever sent. These small games exist to make those ideas something you can feel, not just read.

🪙Guess the Entropy

A coin is minted with a secret bias. Flip it, feel how predictable it is, then put a number — in bits — on your hunch. The gentlest possible introduction to entropy.
